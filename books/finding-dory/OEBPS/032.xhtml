Holding Dory, Hank
 stretched himself across the
 windshield. The two institute workers
 inside the truck jumped out. Hank took
 the driver’s seat and set Dory in a cup.
 He steered while Dory
 navigated.

Dory knew they had to get back to
 the ocean. Nervously, Dory looked at
 Hank. She was going to ask him to do
 something crazy.

"I'm okay with crazy,"
 Hank said, smiling.

Together, they drove the truck off
 the road and into the ocean! Dory, Hank,
 and the rest of the fish fell into the ocean,
 where Dory’s friends and family were
 waiting. They were free!
