Dory told Hank that she couldn’t go to Cleveland. She had to get to the “Jewel of
 Morro Bay, California” to find her family.

Hank looked at her, confused. “That’s this place,” he explained. “The Marine Life
 Institute. The 'Jewel of Morro Bay, California.' You’re here.”

Dory looked around. “I’m from here!” she gasped.

Hank asked her what exhibit she was from, but Dory couldn’t remember. The
 octopus offered her a deal. If he helped Dory find her family, she would give him her
 tag so that Hank could go to Cleveland in her place. Dory agreed and the pair took off.
