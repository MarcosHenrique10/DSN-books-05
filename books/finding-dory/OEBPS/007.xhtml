Then one day, Dory saw a boat pass overhead. Nearby, she heard a
 panicked voice.

“They took my son!” someone cried out. "My son! Help me!
 Please!"

The voice was coming from a clownfish named Marlin. He was looking
 for a boat that had taken his son, Nemo.

Dory knew which way to go.

"Hey, I've seen a boat!" she told him. “Follow me!”

Together, Dory and Marlin traveled across the ocean and brought
 Nemo home.
