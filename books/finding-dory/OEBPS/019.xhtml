Meanwhile, out in the ocean, Marlin and Nemo were looking for a way to
 save Dory when they met two sea lions. The sea lions explained that the
 boat would have taken Dory into the Marine Life Institute, where she would be
 put in Quarantine.

Marlin was worried. How would he and Nemo get inside to save Dory?

The sea lions called for Becky, a bird who lived nearby. Becky scooped
 up Marlin and Nemo in a bucket and headed for the institute. But Becky got distracted and left the bucket in a tree!

Thinking they had been abandoned, Marlin tipped the bucket over. He and Nemo fell out and landed in an aquarium in the institute’s gift shop.

Marlin looked around. “Well, at least we’re not stuck in a bucket anymore,”
 he said, relieved.
