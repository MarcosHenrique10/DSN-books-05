Dory’s parents explained that when they didn’t find her in Quarantine, they realized
 that she must have gone through the pipes to the ocean. They knew they had to do the
 same thing. They had been waiting in the same spot for years, hoping Dory
 would return. When her parents asked her if she had been alone all the time, Dory suddenly
 remembered her friends. Marlin and Nemo were still on the truck!
