The humans dropped Dory into a fish tank and clipped an orange tag to her
 fin. Suddenly, an octopus popped out of the sink next to her. His name was Hank.
 He was actually a septopus, an octopus with seven tentacles. Hank told Dory
 that she was in Quarantine, where sick animals were treated before being released
 back into the ocean. Then he explained what her tag meant. “It’s a transport tag
 for fish who can’t cut it inside the institute,” he said. “They get transferred to
 permanent digs. An aquarium. In Cleveland.”
