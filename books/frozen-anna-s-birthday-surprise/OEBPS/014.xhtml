“No, the best is still ahead!” Elsa insisted.
She led Anna to Oaken’s shop, where she
presented her with one of Oaken’s beautiful
heavy cloaks.

Oaken stepped out of the sauna and noticed
Elsa sneezing. “How about a medical remedy
of my own invention?” he asked.

Anna accepted the bottle of medicine
gratefully. “I think we’re going to need that.”
