More tiny snowmen
appeared and ran into the
courtyard. Kristoff, Sven, and
Olaf tried to keep things under
control, but snowmen were
everywhere!

Kristoff spotted several
little snowmen climbing up to
the cake. He grabbed Olaf’s
head like a bowling ball and
took aim. “Incoming!” Olaf
called out, as he rolled into
the snowmen, knocking them
away from the cake.
