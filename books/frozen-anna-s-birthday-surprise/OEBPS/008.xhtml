Elsa tiptoed into Anna’s room and gently shook her
sister. “Wake up, sleepyhead. It’s your birthday!”

Anna sat up and mumbled, “It’s a birthdaaa . . .” She
fell back onto her bed, snoring.

“Not a birthday. Your birthday!” Elsa replied. “You’ve
never had a real birthday before, so we’re going to make
this one extra great!”
