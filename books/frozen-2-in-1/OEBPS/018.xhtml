Elsa was alone, but she was finally free to use her power.
She had never felt so happy! Then Anna arrived with terrible
news. Elsa had frozen the entire kingdom. Anna wanted Elsa to
unfreeze it, but she didn’t know how! Suddenly, Elsa lost control
of her magic and accidentally hit Anna with an icy blast! Elsa
wished Anna had listened when Elsa told her to leave, then none
of this would have happened.
