This is Elsa, Queen of Arendelle. Her
coronation did not go as planned, and she blamed it all on
her sister, Anna. Anna pulled off Elsa’s glove while they were
arguing about Anna’s engagement to Prince Hans, and Elsa
accidentally froze everything in sight! Elsa had kept her
magical power a secret for years, and now everyone in the
kingdom thought she was dangerous.

So she fled to the North Mountain, where she built a
magnificent ice palace.
