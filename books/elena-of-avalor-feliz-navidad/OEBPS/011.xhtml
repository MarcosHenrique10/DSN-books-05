Armando pops in. “Um, Princess Elena, you
 have a few visitors. Well, more than a few.”

Elena’s friends and subjects from all over Avalor have
 come to invite her to their Navidad celebrations. Naomi
 says there will be a festival at the harbor; Mateo asks her
 to join his family in their village; the merchants want
 Elena to celebrate with them in the town square.

“But our family always spends the holidays together at
 the castle,” says her grandfather, Francisco.

“I wish I could celebrate with all of you. But I can’t be
 five places at once!” says Elena.
