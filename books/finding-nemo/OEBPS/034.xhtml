Back in Sydney, Gill and the gang were determined to reach
 the ocean.

“I don’t understand it,” said the dentist when the Aquascum
 2003 broke. The tank was filthy, so he placed the fish in plastic
 bags. When the dentist wasn’t looking, they escaped!

“Yay! We did it!” they cheered as they floated on the surface.

“Now what?” asked one of the fish. It was time to
 think of a new plan!
