Outside their home, Coral spotted a
 hungry barracuda looking at her eggs. She
 quickly swam to save them!

“No!” Marlin cried, dashing to stop her.

But it was too late. In the blink of an eye,
 the barracuda got what it came for—and
 Coral, too. Only one injured egg was left of
 Marlin’s family.

With a heavy heart, Marlin held the egg
 in his fins and said, “I promise, I will never
 let anything happen to you . . . Nemo.”
