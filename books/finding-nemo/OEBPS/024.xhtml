The next day, the Tank Gang woke up to a clean tank. The
 dentist had installed an Aquascum 2003—the best filter around.
 He scooped up Nemo and placed him in a plastic bag for Darla’s
 birthday present.

Nemo remembered something Gill once said: all drains lead
 to the ocean. If Nemo pretended to be dead, the dentist would
 send him down the drain. He would be back home in the ocean
 in no time.
