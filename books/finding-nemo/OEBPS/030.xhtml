Without Marlin, Dory couldn’t remember where she was or
 how she got there. Even when Nemo popped out from a nearby
 pipe and introduced himself, she didn’t recognize the name.

“Where are we, anyways?” asked Nemo.

“Sydney. . . . P. Sherman, 42 Wallaby Way, Sydney!” she cried,
 suddenly remembering all that had happened. “Nemo! It’s you!”
