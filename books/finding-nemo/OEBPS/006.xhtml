Soon Nemo was ready for his first day of school. But Marlin
 was not. He didn’t want to let Nemo out of his sight.

“What’s one thing we have to remember about the ocean?”
 Marlin asked Nemo.

“It’s not safe,” Nemo replied. He had heard this question a
 million times.

So when Marlin learned that the class was swimming to the
 Drop-off, he panicked and went to find his son. He did not want
 Nemo near the open water.
