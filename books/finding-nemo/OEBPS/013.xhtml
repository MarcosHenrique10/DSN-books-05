That night, Nemo swam through a stream of bubbles called

Soon after his initiation, Nemo learned he was in danger.

 birthday. And she had a history of making her presents go . . .
 belly-up. This year, Nemo would be her gift.

“We can’t send him off to his death,” Gill said. He had a plan.
