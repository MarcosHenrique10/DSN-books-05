All of the fish began swimming down.

“Keep swimming! Just keep swimming!” Marlin instructed.
 It worked! Dory and the other fish escaped from the net, but
 Nemo was trapped underneath it.

“It’s okay. Daddy’s here. Daddy’s got you,” Marlin said as he
 rushed to Nemo’s side.

“Dad?” Nemo said weakly. “I don’t hate you.” Slowly he
 gained back his strength.

Together, Marlin, Dory, and Nemo made their way back
 home.
