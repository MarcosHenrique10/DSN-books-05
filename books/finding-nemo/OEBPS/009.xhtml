Just as Nemo began to swim back, a diver
 appeared behind him and trapped him inside a net!

“Dad! Help me!” Nemo cried. He couldn’t escape.

The diver took Nemo back to the boat and sped
 off, accidentally knocking his scuba mask overboard.

Marlin swam after the boat as quickly as he could,
 but he wasn’t fast enough. Marlin lost sight of the
 boat and his son.
