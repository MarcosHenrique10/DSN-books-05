Outside in the harbor, Marlin thought about Nemo.
 “It’s over, Dory,” he said. “We were too late. I’m going
 home now.” With a heavy heart, he slowly started
 swimming back home. Alone.

“No, no, you can’t!” Dory cried when Marlin turned
 around. “I look at you and . . . I’m home. Please,” she
 begged. Marlin was her only friend.

But Marlin was too heartbroken. He swam away.
