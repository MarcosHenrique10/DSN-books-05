As Marlin and Dory swam toward Sydney, they bumped into
 a jellyfish.

“Come here, little Squishy,” said Dory. But then it stung her.

Suddenly, a forest of jellyfish appeared! Marlin knew they
 were in danger.

To get through the jellyfish safely, Marlin suggested a
 friendly race. In a flash, they were zipping through the jellyfish
 and dodging deadly tentacles to reach the finish.

But both fish got hurt. As Marlin’s eyes began to close, he
 saw a giant sea turtle in the distance. Then everything went
 black.
