Outside Sydney, Marlin was beginning to think they were
 lost. “I have definitely seen this floating speck before,” he said.

In the distance, a huge whale was swimming toward them.

“CAN-YOOOOOUU-GIIIIIIIVE-USSSS-DIRECTIOOOOONS?”
 Dory asked in her best whale voice. When the whale didn’t
 answer, she wasn’t so sure it was friendly.

Then, with little warning, the whale sucked them into its
 mouth.
