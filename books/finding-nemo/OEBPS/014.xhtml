Deep in the ocean, Marlin and Dory swam into a dark canyon
 after the mask. When they reached the bottom, a faint light
 appeared. As the two friends got closer to the light, a scary fish
 with long, sharp teeth appeared.

They swam away as fast as they could—but Dory spotted the
 mask.

“Read it!” Marlin cried, trying to distract the fish.

“‘P. Sherman . . .’” she said, slowly making out the words. “‘42
 Wallaby Way, Sydney.’” They finally knew where Nemo was!
