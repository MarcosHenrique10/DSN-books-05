At the Drop-off, Marlin saw Nemo and his friends
 playing a dangerous game: they were seeing who
 could swim closest to a boat in the open water!

“Nemo, no!” Marlin cried. “You think you can
 do these things, but you just can’t, Nemo!”

Nemo was embarrassed. “I hate you,” he said.
 He swam out to the boat and touched it with his fin.
