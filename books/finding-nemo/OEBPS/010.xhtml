Marlin felt helpless until he
 bumped into a friendly fish named
 Dory. She had seen where the boat went,
 but Dory didn’t have the greatest memory in the
 sea. “I forget things almost instantly,” she explained.
 “It runs in my family . . . or at least I think it does.”

Marlin turned to swim away, but he bumped into a
 shark named Bruce! Bruce invited Marlin and Dory to
 a little “get-together” at an old shipyard nearby.
