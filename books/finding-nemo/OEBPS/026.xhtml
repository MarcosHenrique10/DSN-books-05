Darla had just arrived. She was ready for a teeth cleaning . . .
 and for her birthday present.

“Yay! Fishy, fishy, fishy!” she cheered.

Suddenly, Nigel, Marlin, and Dory stormed into the office
 through a window.

“Hey! I found his dad!” Nigel cried. But Marlin saw Nemo in
 the plastic bag—belly-up.

“Nemo . . .” he gasped. They were too late, he thought. Nigel
 took them back to the harbor.
