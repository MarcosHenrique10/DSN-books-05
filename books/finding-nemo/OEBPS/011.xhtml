Marlin was sure it was a trap, but the fish didn’t really have
 a choice. They followed Bruce to an old sunken ship where two
 other sharks were waiting.

In the shipyard, Marlin spotted the scuba mask. There were
 words on the strap—a clue!

But when Bruce got hungry, he attacked Dory and Marlin.
 He chased them through the shipyard. Dory and Marlin barely
 escaped, and in the rush, Dory accidentally dropped the mask—
their only clue—into the deep, dark water below.
