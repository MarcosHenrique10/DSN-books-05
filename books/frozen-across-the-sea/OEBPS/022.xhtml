“Take us to the kingdom,” Elsa said firmly.

As the Duke led the sisters into the village, they felt
as though they were stepping into a hot, sticky cloud.
The Vakrettans were sprawled out, sweaty and tired.

“Whoa,” Anna remarked.

Elsa didn’t feel one bit shy. She knew she had to
help cool these folks down. After conjuring some snow
clouds, Elsa saw the townsfolk start to come to life.
