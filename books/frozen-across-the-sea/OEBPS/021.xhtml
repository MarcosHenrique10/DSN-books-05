The Duke smoothed his coat as Anna and Elsa got off
the ship. “I am visiting my mother’s cousin’s wife’s nephew
if you must know. Although I wish I hadn’t. If I were you,
I would turn your ship around right now.”

The sisters looked at one another.

The Duke sighed, exasperated. “Vakretta is having the
hottest summer in years. Of course, you wouldn’t care
about that.”
