Soon the ship arrived at its first port: the kingdom of
Zaria. The Zarians clapped and cheered at the sight of
their visitors.

“Welcome, Queen Elsa and Princess Anna!”
King Stebor called in a booming voice.

“We cannot wait to show you our kingdom,” Queen
Renalia added warmly.

“Thank you!” Anna and Elsa said together,
bowing to their hosts.
