When Elena finds Isabel in her room, she can tell something’s wrong.
 “You’re missing the celebration,” she says, handing Isabel a piece of pan dulce.

“It’s almost as good as Mami’s,” Isabel says, taking a bite. Then her face
 falls. “I miss Mami and Papi.”

“I do, too,” says Elena. “That’s why we celebrate every year. If we keep the
 memories alive, it’s like they’re always with us.”

Isabel brightens. “I never thought of it like that.”
