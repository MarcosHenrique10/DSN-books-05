On Día de los Muertos in Avalor, everyone dresses up in costumes and goes to
 the graveyard to build altars to their ancestors. It’s a happy festival for the whole
 family—those who are gone, and those who are still around to remember them.

This year, Elena and her family are setting up an altar to welcome
 the spirits of her mother and father. They will decorate it with flowers,
 pictures, and her parents’ favorite foods.
