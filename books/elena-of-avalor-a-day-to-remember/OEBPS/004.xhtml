Monster pumpkin, coming through!” calls Elena, carrying a giant
 pumpkin into the palace kitchen. Elena’s grandmother is busy baking
 sweet treats for today’s Día de los Muertos celebration.

Día de los Muertos is one of Elena’s favorite holidays. “Today we get
 to celebrate the family members who aren’t with us anymore,” she says.
 “It’s like we’re throwing a party, and everyone we love is invited!”
