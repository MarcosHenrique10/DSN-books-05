“Wait, Doña Angelica!” Elena cries. “I think I know how to fix things. You and
 Carmen are going to cook together!”

Thankfully, Doña Angelica knows all her recipes by heart. So she recites each step to
 Elena, who passes her words along to Carmen.

“It’s like my abuelita is actually here with me,” Carmen says, feeling hopeful.

“She is,” Elena says with a smile.

And with the help of her grandmother, Carmen creates a
 delicious paella.
