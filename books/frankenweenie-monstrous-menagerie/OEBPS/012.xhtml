Victor’s Weird Week, part 2

Victor knew he couldn’t live without his best friend, so
he dug Sparky up and brought him to his laboratory. He
used lightning to bring his dog back to life. At first, Sparky
didn’t move, but after a few seconds, he leaped up and
licked Victor’s face! The experiment had worked! Victor
tried to hide his homesewn creation in his attic. But then
Sparky got out, and the neighborhood would never be
the same. . . .
