Day of
the Turtle
Monster!

Toshiaki uncovers Victor’s secrets
of reanimation and is sure he will
win the school science fair. But when
he digs up his dead turtle Shelley and
conducts the experiment, it goes
terribly wrong! The resurrected
turtle roars to life and grows to
an enormous size—turning into a
gigantic green-shelled monster! Toshiaki
can only get out of the way as the Turtle
Monster heads off towards the town center,
crushing every flower garden and picket
fence in his path!
