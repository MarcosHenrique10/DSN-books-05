Victor’s Weird Week, part 1

One day in school, Mr. Rzykruski taught Victor and his
classmates about the power of electricity. He attached
some electrodes to a frog’s leg, and with a flick of a switch,
electricity ran through the electrodes and the leg twitched!
He also mentioned a science fair, and Victor thought about
entering . . . but soon, the fair was the last thing on Victor’s
mind. His beloved dog Sparky was hit by a car and died.
