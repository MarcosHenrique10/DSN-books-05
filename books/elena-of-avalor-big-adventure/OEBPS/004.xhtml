igs the jaquin flies high over the city of Avalor with
a bagful of his favorite snack—coconuts! Suddenly,
Luna and Skylar zoom by and snatch the bag!

“Hey! Get your own lunch!” Migs shouts.

“We just did!” Skylar calls, chuckling.

But the bag is so heavy Skylar accidentally drops it!
The coconuts fall on a statue in the town square, which topples
over and creates a huge hole in the ground!
